What the current sample suggests

    Metric notes: Churn rate = churned ÷ all customers. Retention rate = retained ÷ all customers. Revenue at risk is a simple monthly recurring-charge exposure proxy, not a forecast or lifetime revenue estimate. Average tenure is the arithmetic mean of TenureMonths. Filters recalculate all KPIs and charts.
    Dataset limitation: 15 synthetic customer records only. Findings are descriptive, not causal or statistically generalisable. Small-sample patterns should not be used as business forecasts.
