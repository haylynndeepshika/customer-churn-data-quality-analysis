# Executive Analytics Capstone

This presentation combines the outputs of Tasks 1–3 into an executive-facing customer churn analytics summary.

## Contents
1. Executive summary and KPIs
2. Dataset and business question
3. Data quality workflow
4. Churn and retention measures
5. Contract-level descriptive patterns
6. Customer segmentation and transparent risk score
7. Recommendations and expected impact to test
8. Limitations and validation plan

## Important caveats
The dataset contains 15 synthetic records. Results are descriptive only. The rule-based risk score is not a churn probability and is not statistically calibrated. Recommendations are hypotheses to test. No quantified business impact is claimed.
